# Showcase Revision 14 — design scope

Revision 14 is a presentation-only redesign of the public GitHub Pages showcase.

Research software version remains `0.4.0`.

The redesign is constrained by a Mastermind-style mechanism review of ten reference sites: Karimoku Research, Neko Health, Bisou Gallery, Living House, The Beautiful Truth, Slate + Ash, Whanganui UNESCO City of Design, Mennour, Angela Ricciardi, and Standard Projects.

Transferable mechanisms admitted into R14:

1. editorial scale instead of dashboard density;
2. negative space and long-form pacing instead of card accumulation;
3. serif/sans typographic contrast using local/system font stacks only;
4. archive/index structures for evidence and modules;
5. visuals that explain the actual research system rather than decorative imagery;
6. restrained high-contrast palette with one functional accent;
7. asymmetry and large image/diagram fields without hiding navigation or evidence boundaries;
8. one deliberate dark contrast section rather than an all-dark SaaS shell.

Explicit exclusions:

- no remote fonts or runtime assets;
- no generic gradient-orb, glassmorphism, floating-3D, or AI-generated hero imagery;
- no new behavioral-efficacy claim;
- no protocol, benchmark-result, runtime, or scientific-evidence version change;
- no copying of proprietary artwork, photography, layouts, or brand assets from reference sites.
