# Rookframe Research / BASTION-01 Brand Architecture

## Status

**Rookframe Research** is the working identity for a planned independent research
company. It is not represented here as an incorporated entity or registered
trademark. **BASTION-01** is the first public product line under that working
identity and is released free and open source under the MIT License.

This document is the naming authority for public portfolio, grant, website, and
release copy. Technical identifiers remain governed by their existing runtime and
compatibility contracts.

## Company

### Rookframe Research

**Mission:** build open research systems that make AI-assisted inquiry more
inspectable, falsifiable, reproducible, and useful to independent researchers.

**Position:** a research-systems company, not a model vendor and not a consultancy
claiming scientific conclusions from software checks.

The name combines two ideas: a **rook** protects lines and constrains movement;
a **frame** carries load while leaving components replaceable. The result is a
systems-engineering identity centered on structure, protection, and inspectable
modularity rather than military role-play.

## Product Line 01

### BASTION-01

**Category:** open evidence-control system for AI-assisted research and engineering.

**One-sentence description:** BASTION-01 turns a complex task into explicit
evidence obligations, routes each obligation to a specialist method, preserves
receipts and uncertainty, and keeps canonical release under separate authority.

**Public promise:** evidence before confidence; verification before release.

`BASTION-01` is the public product name. `The-Gauntlet`, `the-gauntlet`,
`gauntlet`, `.gauntlet.json`, `.egrt/`, and `egrt/*` are stable technical or
historical identifiers until a future explicitly breaking migration.

## Voice and presentation

- Lead with the research question, released mechanism, evidence state, and next
  falsifiable study.
- Prefer precise nouns such as system, module, receipt, protocol, evaluation,
  and boundary over fantasy or combat metaphors.
- Do not call the modules Gems, Stones, or supernatural entities in grant-facing
  copy. Those words may appear only when documenting legacy aliases or historical
  experiments.
- Never imply that a passing check establishes scientific efficacy, that a
  multi-agent vote is independent evidence, or that the planned company already
  has legal status.
- Pair every strong claim with a direct path to source, method, receipt, or an
  explicit `NOT_MEASURED` / unresolved state.

## Compatibility policy

This release is a display-name migration. It intentionally does not rename:

- the GitHub repository or Pages URL;
- the Python distribution, CLI entrypoint, import package, or protocol schemas;
- skill directories, frontmatter IDs, slash commands, environment variables,
  runtime state directories, receipt fields, or historical benchmark conditions;
- existing FOIL- or Mirror-named artifact paths that external links may use.

The canonical adaptive-system document is [`COUNTERFORM.md`](COUNTERFORM.md).
[`MIRROR.md`](MIRROR.md) remains a compatibility locator, and `foil` remains the
technical namespace.

## Reference pattern

The identity architecture was informed by a representative scan of established
research organizations and research-software publishers, including
[Arc Institute](https://arcinstitute.org/),
[Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/research/),
[Anthropic Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research),
[OpenAI Research](https://openai.com/research/),
[Ai2](https://allenai.org/),
[Broad Institute](https://www.broadinstitute.org/),
[Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/),
[IBM Research](https://research.ibm.com/),
[NVIDIA Research](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/research/),
[Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/),
[Mila](https://mila.quebec/en),
[The Alan Turing Institute](https://www.turing.ac.uk/), and the
[Flatiron Institute](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/flatiron/).

The transferable pattern is structural: short institutional name, direct
mission, compact product names, and immediate access to research outputs and
evidence. No claim is made that this is an objective ranking of research
organizations.

## Preliminary collision screen

Exact-name web and GitHub searches performed on 2026-09-01 found no active
research/software brand using **Rookframe Research** or **Rookframe Bastion**.
The screen rejected **Parallax** and **Proofline** as module/product candidates
because active AI/software projects already use them; generic uses of
**Bastion** also exist, which is why the public product is always styled
**Rookframe Research BASTION-01** or **BASTION-01 by Rookframe Research**.

This is a preliminary public-web collision screen, not legal clearance. Before
incorporation, fundraising, trademark filing, or paid distribution, run formal
corporate-name, trademark-class, domain, and counsel review in the intended
jurisdictions.
