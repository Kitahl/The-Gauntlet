THE PERSONALIZED COMPLEMENT

FOIL mirrors the method you are using—then supplies what is missing.

FOIL is not a personality assessment. It treats observed errors as noisy evidence, maintains competing explanations, supplies missing proof or engineering methods, and separates immediate task success from what the user can later do independently.

EPISTEMIC CONTRACT

Claims carry obligations.

The system is designed to distinguish source provenance from certainty and to keep unsupported claims visibly unresolved.

MEASURED

Observed or computed

Record the method, artifact, run, and scope.

CITED

Source-entailing

The inspected source must support the exact proposition, not a nearby one.

DERIVED

Premise-linked

Explicit inference from ledgered premises and assumptions.

UNKNOWN

Still unresolved

Missing proof or evidence stays visible instead of being laundered into prose.
