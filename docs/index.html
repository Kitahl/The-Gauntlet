ARCHITECTURE

A control plane plus specialist research modules.

The Research Orchestrator decomposes a task by epistemic obligation. Specialist modules return bounded findings; evidence and verifier fit govern synthesis rather than votes, confidence, or agent count.

ADAPTIVE COMPLEMENT

FOIL supplies the missing method without turning one error into a personality label.

FOIL — Adaptive Reasoning Complement — maintains competing explanations for observed performance, conditions evidence on assistance and context, and separates immediate joint task success from later independent competence.

Human authority governs voluntary decisions.
Evidence governs factual warrant.

RESEARCH MODULES

Each module owns a distinct evidence obligation.

CONTROL

Research Orchestrator

Frame, decompose, route, integrate, audit, and release.

 FORMAL

Formal Reasoning

Proof, logic, probability/statistics, optimization, countermodels.

 DISCOVERY

Research Discovery

Existing tools, literature, code, and cross-domain anchors.

 SYNTHESIS

Method Synthesis

New mechanisms only after existing methods fail a named constraint.

 ENGINEERING

Engineering Verification

Architecture, implementation, integration, execution, and software verification.

 EVALUATION

Evaluation & Benchmarking

Baselines, capability measurement, ceilings, cost, and stop decisions.

 ASSURANCE

Process Assurance Framework

Audit frames, process, inherited assumptions, stale state, and false-green verification.

 PREFLIGHT

Decision Preflight Protocol

Artifact-grounded orientation before consequential action or after failure.

 REVIEW

Evidence Review Panel

Selective independent perspectives with evidence packs and control comparison.
