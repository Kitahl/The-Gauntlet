01 / Purpose

Competing explanations. Discriminating evidence.

Strong Inference coordinates specialist methods around the epistemic obligation of the task: what must be searched, proved, built, measured, challenged, or left explicitly unresolved.

Evidence-governedClaims stay attached to the kind of support they actually have.
Mechanically inspectableRuntime checks, source, tests, and research receipts remain public.
Compatibility-preservingThe product interface can change while stable technical IDs and commands do not.

02 / Workflows

Five recurring technical workflows.

The suite routes work by obligation rather than by personality or a single universal prompt.

01

Establish

Frame the claim, identify the required warrant, and decide whether the task needs proof, current evidence, execution, or measurement.

02

Discover

Search literature, prior art, software, and current sources before novelty is asserted or a mechanism is reinvented.

03

Construct

Formalize, synthesize, implement, and test the smallest mechanism that can resolve the named constraint.

04

Challenge

Use competing hypotheses, independent review, counterexamples, controls, ablations, and process assurance against persuasive false greens.

05

Release

Separate supported conclusions from open state, preserve reproducibility, and expose the evidence boundary.

04 / Coordinated system

Route at the center. Four control surfaces around it.

The specialist modules do work. The control modules decide how that work is framed, checked, independently reviewed, and adapted to the task.

Route · Orchestration`soul` / `/soul` — frame, decompose, route, integrate, release.
Adapt · Adaptive Complement`foil` / `/foil` — task-specific complementary assistance and calibration.
Assure · Process Assurance`infinity-gauntlet` / `/gauntlet` — stale-state, frame, boundary, and false-green checks.
Preflight · Decision Preflight`meditate` — grounding before consequential decisions and after failures.
Review · Evidence Review`council-of-elders` / `/council` — selective independent evidence and method review.
