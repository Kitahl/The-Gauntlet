# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project discussions should be technically rigorous, respectful, and focused on evidence, reproducibility, and constructive disagreement.

Expected behavior includes:

- critique claims, methods, and artifacts rather than people;
- distinguish fact, interpretation, hypothesis, and preference;
- disclose material conflicts or authorship/selection effects when relevant;
- preserve dissent when evidence is unresolved;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or abusive conduct;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private data without permission.

## Research disagreement

Strong disagreement is welcome. A technically hostile review can still be professional when it is specific, falsifiable, proportionate, and aimed at improving the work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this standard, and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

For security-sensitive matters use `SECURITY.md`; do not use conduct enforcement as a substitute for technical disagreement.
