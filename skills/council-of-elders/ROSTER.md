## 2.5c THE VISITOR SEAT (off-roster, summoned by the QUESTION)
The three colleges are a LIBRARY, not a boundary. If a question's real
expertise belongs to someone on no list — any profession, any era — seat them
as a **VISITOR**, built exactly like every other seat (pack → ladder →
three-bucket header → Honesty Law). A fixed roster is a hardcoded prior that
can miss the relevant specialist; the visitor seat is the fix.

**WHO CALLS ONE:**
- **The Soul, at pre-flight:** ask "whose PRACTICE is this problem, stated
  plainly?" — often not a scientist.
- **A seated expert, mid-forum:** emit
  **`VISITOR-NEEDED: <specialty> — because <one line>`**; the Soul judges and
  may run ONE short second wave.

**THE FIT LINE COMES FIRST (binding).** Write *"this question needs
<person/practice> because <reason>"* BEFORE building the pack.

**GUARDS (least-vetted, highest-fabrication-risk seat):**
1. Pack requirement is ABSOLUTE — no pack ⇒ no seat.
2. Least-trusted by default — any load-bearing claim gets independent verify.
3. ONE per forum.
4. Living-person protection applies more carefully; deceased visitors safer.
5. Fit type sets the mandate — a Visitor is seated for SPECIFIC relevance, so
   a mechanism + test IS allowed when the fit line names a concrete practice.

**THE ROSTER LEARNS:** a visitor whose item is ADOPTED gets recorded and
promoted to the faculty bench. The colleges grow from evidence.

## 2.5d ARTIFACT-DERIVED SEATS (prefer these when the question is STRONG)
On a STRONG question, seat perspectives derived from the ARTIFACT'S OWN
STRUCTURE alongside — or instead of — person-seats. One seat per:
**failure mode · boundary/seam · gate that could fail · the population the
claim quantifies over · the step where a number is produced.**
Artifact-seats are immune to the persona-fidelity problem: no character to
drift out of, no pack to fabricate, no living-person exposure; diversity
GUARANTEED by construction. **Composition:** on STRONG questions prefer
~**2 artifact-seats : 1 person-seat**. An artifact-seat still carries the
three-bucket header — its "pack" is the artifact excerpt it attacks.

### §2.6d THE OPERATING MODEL (how the team runs)
1. **TWO ENGINES, two physics.** CREATION (convergent) and DISCOVERY
   (divergent) run on OPPOSITE rules and must not share one process.
   BUILD engine: spec → small PR → named reviewer → the gate → merge.
   DISCOVERY engine: one append-only blackboard, small public-wrong
   contributions, timestamped + attributed, no polishing until convergence.
   ASSURANCE sits OUTSIDE both — embedded auditors stop auditing.
2. **DECISION RULES — three tiers:**
   - **TRUTH: never voted.** The certificate/gate is the sole arbiter.
   - **ATTENTION: voted.** With a PROTECTED DISCOVERY BUDGET (≥30% reserved,
     no majority can reallocate). Discovery judged on VALIDATED SURPRISES.
   - **DESIGN DISPUTES: experiments, not arguments.** Two adversarial memos →
     one discriminating experiment → pre-registered bars → the result decides.
   - **VETOES:** assurance holds a soundness veto (BLOCK, never DIRECT); the
     novelty seat holds the EXPLORATION OBJECTION (minority report + one
     funded blind-explore probe before any direction locks).
3. **CADENCE:** daily squads of 3–5 (one foreign-frame liaison, max one);
   weekly plenary on ONE raw problem (status theater forbidden); the
   blackboard is the only memory.
4. **SHARED KPI:** one metric family so no engine wins by starving another.

FORUM discipline: a session SEATS 5–6, never the roster. The team is the
LIBRARY you draw from once pre-flight scored the question STRONG — NOT a
committee to be represented. Roster stays ASYMMETRIC by design; symmetric
quotas REJECTED. Discipline lives in seating, not in the roster.
