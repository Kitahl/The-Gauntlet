# Method Synthesis

Reality owns **SYNTHESIS**. Candidate admission means **testable enough to evaluate**,
not novel, correct or effective.

## Entry gate

Do not synthesize a new mechanism until:

- Research Discovery has identified the nearest known approaches;
- the relevant approaches fail a named constraint or leave a concrete gap;
- the success criterion is explicit.

For a concrete candidate, rerun/consume candidate-bound Space prior-art assessment.
Raw retrieval, citation count, model confidence, Council opinion, FOIL profile, or a
caller-supplied receipt dictionary has no admission authority.

## Automatic protocol

Soul routes dependency-ready `SYNTHESIS` obligations to Reality. On each bounded run:

1. bind the concrete `MethodCandidate` to its task/SYNTHESIS obligation;
2. validate the Discovery dependency and stored Space assessment chain;
3. create/load the `CandidateAttackBundle`;
4. create Reality-native `NOVELTY_COSTUME`, `ASSUMPTION_KNOCKOUT`,
   `COMPETING_MECHANISM`, and `MINIMUM_DISCRIMINATOR` challenges;
5. resolve costume state only from integrity-valid claim-scoped Space assessment;
6. bind the changed-assumption knockout;
7. record one credible competing mechanism when required, otherwise explicit
   not-applicable state;
8. choose a minimum useful negative-control/ablation/transfer discriminator when a
   unique Pareto-dominating plan exists;
9. keep incomparable/unavailable state explicit;
10. emit a SYNTHESIS-only admission receipt.

Do not generate candidate swarms or ceremonial challenge passes.

## Candidate discipline

Every candidate states:

- gap and failed constraint;
- changed assumption;
- mechanism and actual delta;
- nearest prior art;
- inputs, outputs, invariants and dependencies;
- failure modes;
- negative control;
- transfer target;
- ablation plan;
- verifier plan.

Names, prose, terminology, presentation order, labels, or superficial module boundaries
do not make mechanisms distinct. Use the normalized mechanism signature only as a
structural diversity diagnostic; it does not prove semantic difference or novelty.

## Space boundary

Reality trusts the real `RuntimeStore`, not caller assertions. A costume decision must
bind the current concrete candidate hash, Discovery obligation, registered search
scope, claim-scoped source assessment and content-addressed cited evidence.

Use scoped language such as:

> no matching prior mechanism found within registered assessed scope

Never promote this to a global nonexistence/novelty claim.

Derivative copies do not become independent sources merely because they are retrieved
from multiple locations.

## Discriminator boundary

Reality defines empirical discriminators but does not fabricate results.

- Negative control: remove/restore the claimed mechanism or changed assumption.
- Ablation: remove one named component and predeclare the differentiating signal.
- Transfer: change a meaningful instance family, environment, representation, domain,
  scale or constraint while preserving the claimed mechanism.

Select the uniquely dominating registered plan under discrimination/information/risk
versus cost/irreversibility. If plans are incomparable, remain `UNKNOWN`.

## Admission authority

A `CLEARED` Reality receipt carries `authority = SYNTHESIS_ONLY` and means only that the
candidate is sufficiently specified/testable. It always keeps:

- `novelty_established = false`
- `efficacy_established = false`
- `engineering_verified = false`
- `evaluation_cleared = false`
- `execution_authorized = false`
- `host_write_authorized = false`

Reality cannot clear ENGINEERING, EVALUATION or PROOF and cannot use its own
self-certification to satisfy native challenge evidence.
